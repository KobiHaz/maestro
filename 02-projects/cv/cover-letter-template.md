# CV — Cover Letter Template

Use this as a starting point. Always customize per role.

---

**[Date]**

Dear [Hiring Manager / Team],

I'm applying for the [Role] position at [Company]. With [X years] building [relevant domain] at [key company], I bring [core value prop].

**[Paragraph 1 — Hook: why this role, why this company]**
[Connect Xsheva / Spinomenal experience to their mission. Be specific about what excites you.]

**[Paragraph 2 — Key proof point 1]**
At Spinomenal, [specific achievement with number]. This is directly relevant to [specific thing in JD].

**[Paragraph 3 — Key proof point 2]**
[Second proof point. Different angle — team leadership, product impact, or technical depth.]

**[Closing]**
I'd love to discuss how [specific skill] can drive [their goal]. Happy to connect at your convenience.

Best,
Kobi Hazout
kobi@leadslords.com

---

## Notes for Claude

- Keep it to 3 short paragraphs + closing — no more
- Mirror keywords from the JD without being obvious
- Lead with the most impressive number you have for that role
- No generic lines ("I am a results-driven professional…")
- End with a specific ask, not "I look forward to hearing from you"
